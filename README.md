# Project Title: 

*Improving Patient Flow in Community Healthcare (with Mental Health Considerations)*

## Extern.com | Extern, Process Design & Strategy Consulting

## Operational Insight

In psychiatric care, efficiency is measured not only by throughput bu by the system's ability to detect and respond to risk before escalation. 

## Problem Statement

What went wrong (delays, inefficiency, silent risk in psychiatric care)

## Objectives

1. Reduce wait times
2. Improve Communication
3. Introduce early risk detection thinking

## Key Insights

- Bottleneck at intake
- Peak-hour staffing mismatch
- Psychiatric care = detection problem not just flow problem

## Approach

- Workflow analysis
- Root cause breakdown
- Proposed solutions (digital check-in, queue system, etc.)

## Results (or Expected Impact)

- 30-40% wait time reduction
- Improved patient satisfaction
- Better staff coordination

## Tools/Skills Highlighted
- Data Analysis
- Process mapping
- Healthcare operations thinking

## Future Work
- Risk Scoring models
- Dashboard development
- Mental health-specific workflow redesign
